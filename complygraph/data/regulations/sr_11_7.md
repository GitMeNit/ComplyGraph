# SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12 - Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management

## Scope and definition of a model
A model is a quantitative method, system or approach that applies statistical, economic, financial or mathematical techniques to turn inputs into quantitative estimates. Model risk arises from fundamental errors in a model or from incorrect or inappropriate use. Machine learning credit scorecards and many AI scoring systems fall in scope.

## Model development, implementation and use
Development requires a clear statement of purpose, sound theory and data, documented assumptions and limitations, testing of the model and its components, and controls on implementation. Use must be consistent with design intent and subject to ongoing review of its limitations.

## Model validation
Validation is an independent review with three core elements: evaluation of conceptual soundness, ongoing monitoring including benchmarking and process verification, and outcomes analysis such as back-testing. Validators need competence, influence and incentives independent of model development. Validation frequency should reflect model risk, and material changes trigger revalidation.

## Effective challenge
Models must be subject to critical analysis by objective, informed parties who can identify limitations and assumptions and produce appropriate change. Challenge depends on competence, independence and organisational standing.

## Governance, policies and controls
The board and senior management set the model risk appetite, approve policies and receive reports. Firms keep a comprehensive model inventory, with documentation detailed enough for an unfamiliar party to understand operation and limitations, and define roles for owners, controllers and users.

## Vendor and third-party models
Vendor models must be validated like internal ones as far as possible. Firms should require sufficient documentation from vendors, run their own testing and outcomes analysis, and have contingency plans if the vendor ceases to support the product.
