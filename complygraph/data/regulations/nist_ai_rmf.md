# NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0

## Govern function
Govern cultivates a risk-aware culture through policies, accountability structures, roles, third-party risk practices and decommissioning processes. It applies across all other functions.

## Map function
Map establishes context: intended purposes, stakeholders, potential positive and negative impacts, and system categorisation, so that risks are identified before measurement and mitigation.

## Measure function
Measure uses quantitative and qualitative methods to analyse and track AI risks, including trustworthiness characteristics such as validity, reliability, safety, security, resilience, transparency, explainability, privacy and fairness with harmful bias managed.

## Manage function
Manage prioritises identified risks and allocates resources to respond, including mitigation, transfer, avoidance or acceptance, plus incident response and communication plans for deployed systems.

## Generative AI profile
The generative AI profile adds risks such as confabulation, information integrity problems, data privacy leakage, prompt injection and information security issues, and suggests actions for provenance tracking, content filtering and human review.
